Hearth for WebOS is a fast, calm home screen for LG webOS TVs (webOS 3 and newer, designed for speed). It replaces the busy LG home with a clock, the weather, your apps and TV inputs in one row, and a plain, picture or slideshow background.

  • Clock, date and weather: sixteen fonts, any position, colours and gradients with adjustable opacity, so it stays readable on light pictures.
  • Fast and smooth: starts almost instantly; the app list and weather refresh quietly in the background.
  • Your apps and inputs: choose what shows, re-order, hide, uninstall; Back opens a Recent apps panel.
  • Wallpaper: plain black, your own picture, a USB slideshow, or the bundled nature photos.
  • Quiet mode and OLED shift: hide the apps when idle, and move pixels slowly to protect OLED screens.
  • Replace LG Home (rooted TVs): add Hearth as a TV input, use the helper script or the SAM method, and map the Home button with Input Hook.

Rooted TV (Homebrew Channel): everything above. Not rooted: the home screen, clock, weather and launching apps work; options that need root are greyed out with the reason.

Reference device: LG firmware 04.60.90 (webOS 7.6.0). Experimental and not tested on a TV: webOS 3, the app list without root, the webOS 11 permission fix. Developed with Claude (Anthropic) and tested on a real TV. Screenshots, credits and full instructions are on the project page.
